Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Information we collect

When you request an estimate or appointment, we collect the information you submit, which may include your name, email address, phone number, vehicle year, make, model and type, service selections, condition notes, service address, preferred dates, and vehicle photos. The owner desk also stores estimate, appointment, and payment status. Stripe processes online card payments; Killer Detailing LLC does not receive or store your full card number through this website.

How information is used

Killer Detailing LLC uses this information to review estimate requests, prepare and send estimates, communicate about requested services, schedule and manage appointments, process deposits through Stripe, and maintain the business calendar. Uploaded photos and request details are made available to the owner through the password-protected owner desk.

Service providers

Website hosting, database and photo storage, payment processing, email, SMS delivery, and calendar services may process information as needed to provide those functions. These providers are not authorized by Killer Detailing LLC to use SMS opt-in data or personal information for their own marketing on our behalf.

Text messages

Automated text alerts are for the Killer Detailing LLC business owner who signs up through the owner text alert form. Alerts report estimate and appointment activity. Customers are not enrolled in SMS by submitting an estimate request. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Information sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact estimaterequest@killerdetailingllc.com.
