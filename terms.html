Estimates and appointments

Submitting an estimate request does not book an appointment. Killer Detailing LLC reviews the information and vehicle photos and provides a custom estimate. After the customer accepts, the customer selects an appointment time and payment method. An online booking is confirmed after successful deposit payment; a cash booking is confirmed after the customer chooses cash. The customer’s estimate and booking page shows the applicable amount and appointment details.

SMS Terms

Killer Detailing LLC sends automated transactional account notifications only to the business owner who signs up using the owner text alert form. Messages may report a new estimate request, an accepted estimate, or a confirmed appointment. Message frequency varies based on website activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help. Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchasing services. No promotional messages are sent through this alert program.
