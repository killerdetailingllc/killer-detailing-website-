Packages

Inside, outside,
or the whole ride.

Tell us which areas need attention. We’ll confirm the work and price in your estimate.

Burial – Outside

A wash and rinse for the body, wheels, and tires, with attention to road grime, bugs, and exterior glass.

Embalming – Interior

Vacuum seats, floors, and mats. Wipe down the dash, console, and door panels, and clean the inside glass. Flag pet hair or spills when you request your estimate.

Viewing – Exterior & Interior

Pair an exterior wash with an interior clean in the same visit. Mention the trunk, hatch, or truck bed if you want it included.
